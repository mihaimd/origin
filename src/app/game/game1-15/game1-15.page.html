~ 🍋 Story Scene: The Stand Beneath the Golden Tree ~

The satchel on your shoulder grows heavier with every step—fruit, meat, herbs, and truths you didn’t ask for. The village around you blurs beneath the weight of new thoughts.

Just when the heat becomes too much—both from the sun and the furnace of your mind—you spot it.

Nestled between the herbalist’s stall and a seller of silks, beneath the canopy of a blooming goldenleaf tree, stands a small lemonade stand. The air around it is cool, touched by the scent of mint, citrus, and the faintest whisper of rain.

A girl about your age stands behind the cart. Her name is Lira, a distant cousin of Mira the greengrower, known more for her clever mind than her conversation. She looks up as you approach, her amber eyes bright in the dappled sunlight.

“ - You look like someone who’s been chasing questions through fire,” she says, offering you a wooden cup filled with pale yellow lemonade and sprigs of silverleaf."

You take a sip. It’s shockingly cold. Sharply sweet. The kind of drink that makes the world stop spinning for just a moment.

“People say I just sell lemonade,” Lira murmurs, leaning on her elbow. “But I steep it in the water from the deepwell spring—the one that touches the roots of the Old Grove.” She lowers her voice. “That water… remembers.”

As you drink, you feel something shift. Not a vision—a clarity. A focus. The sun catches the pendant, and for a flash, you see reflections of yourself in other places. Other lives.

Lira notices the look in your eyes. “ - The spring never lies. Whatever it's showing you… you were meant to see.”

Lira slides a small tray toward you. On it are three flavored droplets, each glimmering like dew, resting in hollowed lemon peels.

“ - Choose one. Each will stir a memory from far, far away… brought to you by the water beneath the world.”

🍊 Sunfruit drop – Memories of foreign lands, travel, and culture
🌿 Mintroot drop – Knowledge of healing, herbalism, and ancient remedies
🫖 Spicebloom drop – Trade, exotic ingredients, and historical cuisine
