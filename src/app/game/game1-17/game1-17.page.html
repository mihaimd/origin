~ 🍬 Story Scene: The Sugar That Whispers ~

The crowd begins to thin as the sun leans westward, casting golden streaks between the market tents. Your satchel is full, your errands complete—fruit, vegetables, meat, and more than a fair share of unspoken truths.

But just as you turn to take the path home, a familiar scent catches your attention. Warm honey. Roasted nuts. Cinnamon. Caramel.

Your feet stop before your thoughts do.

A small, curved cart stands alone under a patchwork parasol. Glass jars and polished tins line its surface, filled with sweets in every shape and color—candied petals, honey cubes, sugar-dusted plums, and twisted licorice like ribbons of night sky.

Behind the stand, humming softly, is Gramma Thistle, a kind-eyed elder in robes the color of violet dusk. She’s lived in Lorynth longer than anyone can remember, though no one seems to recall how she came to be here.

“Ah,” she says without looking up, “the heart seeks sugar when the soul is tired.”

She taps a wooden tray with her ladle. On it are three small confections, each nestled in a flower-shaped wrapper.

“ - Every sweet has a story,” she says, sliding the tray toward you. “And every story, a flavor of truth.”

“You may choose one, but only one. Sweets are stubborn things—they stick to what matters most.”

You eye the three treats laid before you. Each glows faintly, like it remembers something you do not.

🌸 Blushdrop Candy – soft pink with a floral shimmer — Evokes nostalgia and childhood memories
🌑 Nightfruit Chew – dark and star-speckled — Stirs dreams and myth, ancient tales whispered under stars
🌞 Crispfire Caramel – amber with tiny gold flecks — Sparks passion, warmth, tales of courage and legacy
