~ 🔨 “Boy! You there!” ~

Then, another call catches your ear. A voice you recognize.

It’s Tarn, the village stonemason, arms powdered in chalk and lime, standing beside a massive slab of dark slate. He points to you with a grin—too wide to be friendly.

- "You’ve got sharp eyes. Your mother says you’re good with puzzles and parts. Come here—tell me what shape this was meant to be."

You approach. Carved faintly into the stone is a complex spiral, surrounding what looks like the shape of a lock… or a gate. A pattern calls to you —not with sound, but with familiarity.

Tarn lays out three stone fragments, each etched with symbols—none of which you’ve ever seen, and yet… you know exactly what they mean.

Stone of Remembrance

🐉 A shard marked with a compass rose
🕯️ A sliver etched with a crescent moon and eye
⏳ A triangle engraved with overlapping runes
