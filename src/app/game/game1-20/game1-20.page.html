~ 🌿 The Whispering Leaves ~

Morning light filters softly through the cottage window, catching the edge of the pendant as it hangs from your bedpost. It flickers faintly, like it dreamed alongside you.

Downstairs, your mother moves quietly. You smell porridge and rosemary, hear the creak of the kettle, the gentle hum of a lullaby she hasn’t sung since you were small.

You sit down to eat in silence. She watches you with that kind of mother’s look that says everything and asks nothing.

Then she places a small satchel beside your bowl.

“We’re low on heartleaf and thistlebud,” she says gently. “Can you go to Healer Aranel before the dew burns off?”

You nod. A simple task. A familiar one.

Almost comforting.

You sling the satchel over your shoulder and step into the cool air. The village is quieter in the morning, like it’s still remembering itself. You walk a narrow path that winds behind the mill, down into a grove of sun-dappled ash trees.

The healer’s cottage sits nestled beneath a weeping willow, its branches touching the roof like blessings. Vines grow along the stone walls. Tiny bells chime in the windows. You’ve been here many times—but today, everything feels more alive.

The door creaks open before you knock.

Inside, Healer Aranel waits—tall, willow-slim, her hair like a silver braid of moonlight. She’s not old, not young. Not quite part of time at all.

She smiles faintly.

“I thought you'd come,” she says. “Your mother always remembers the right herbs before the winds shift.”

She gestures toward her long worktable, lined with jars, bundles of dried plants, and bowls of pulsing moss.

“But before you take what she needs… perhaps you should take something you need as well.”

She places three bundles of herbs before you, wrapped in silk, each tied with a color-coded thread:

💜 Bundle of Heartleaf and Lavender – Remedies and emotions, the healing of sorrow and spirit
💚 Bundle of Sage and Thornmint – Protection, purification, and knowledge of plants with edge and wisdom
🤍 Bundle of Cloudblossom and White Root – Rare herbs said to enhance memory and dreams
