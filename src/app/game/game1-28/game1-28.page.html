~ ⚡ Story Scene: The Cave That Breathes ~

You choose your path, and the fish scatter—racing ahead like silver comets. You follow them through swaying curtains of kelp and past sunken stones marked by time and water. The light dims as you descend, but your pendant glows brighter with every stroke.

The fish vanish into the shadows ahead, and then… you see it.

The mouth of the cave yawns open like a wound in the lakebed—carved stone overgrown with moss and coral. Strange symbols flicker faintly along its edges, pulsing in time with your heartbeat.

You drift closer, the pressure of the deep tightening around you. Just beyond the entrance, you spot something gleaming—a treasure chest, half-buried in the sand, wrapped in chains of silver and coral.

But before you can move closer, the water trembles.

From behind the chest, something slithers into view—long, thick, and pulsing with streaks of bright blue light. A giant electric eel, easily twice your size, coils around the treasure with eerie grace. Its eyes glow with unnatural intelligence, and arcs of static flicker along its slick body.

It doesn’t strike, but it watches you. Waiting.

You hover there, heart pounding. You know you cannot defeat it with strength. The eel is ancient, powerful, and attuned to the magic of the lake. To reach the treasure, you must be clever—patient—and outthink the guardian.

The pendant warms against your chest. You realize the eel reacts not to movement, but to energy. Too much force, and it surges with lightning. But there must be a way to move without disturbing its current…
