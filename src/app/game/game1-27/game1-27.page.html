~ 🐟 Beneath the Glass Veil ~

You hold the shimmering leaf of deepgill weed in your hand, its silver veins pulsing softly like a heartbeat. With a deep breath, you place it on your tongue and chew.

The taste is sharp—like mint and iron—but as you swallow, a strange warmth spreads through your chest. You inhale once more… and your lungs accept the water like air.

Stepping into the lake, you slip beneath the surface. The world above fades into rippling light, and a profound stillness surrounds you. Threads of sunlight pierce the depths, painting the lakebed with moving lace.

As you descend deeper, you pass drifting moss, fallen branches, and stones worn smooth by time. Suddenly, you find yourself surrounded by a bank of glowing silver fish, their sleek bodies moving in intricate patterns like ink across a page.

The fish dart around you, forming shapes and spirals that seem… purposeful. Not random. You reach out your hand, and the nearest fish splits from the group and swims a perfect circle around you.

Then the bank separates into three distinct schools, each forming a symbol in the water—one a swirling spiral, another a flickering star, the third a sharp arrow pointing downward.

The fish pulse with bioluminescent light, waiting, as if asking you to choose. Each group begins to slowly drift in a different direction—inviting you to follow.

🐚 Follow the Spiral Fish – They swirl toward a hidden underwater garden. Choosing them will trigger a quiz about **aquatic plants and natural ecosystems**.
🌟 Follow the Star Fish – They flash and dart toward a rock formation etched with old markings. Choosing them will trigger a quiz about **mythical sea creatures and ancient underwater lore**.
🪨 Follow the Arrow Fish – They cut swiftly toward the lake floor, guiding you to the cave’s entrance. Choosing them will trigger a quiz about **geography, underwater exploration, and deep-sea mysteries**.
