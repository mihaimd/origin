The moonseed glows faintly in your palm as you approach the edge of the hollow. The air here feels colder, stiller—like the forest is holding its breath.

You kneel at the roots of a gnarled tree, its bark slick with dew, and press the seed into a crevice just above the soil. As it touches the earth, a soft pulse ripples outward, like a heartbeat in the ground. The surrounding shadows ease, just slightly.

The fairy appears again, hovering in silence for a moment. Her wings flutter in slow, deliberate motion.

“You have honored the grove,” she says. “When the time comes, and your path grows dark, I will be there. Not before. Not after. But exactly when you need me.”

She lifts a hand, and a faint silver mark appears just above your wrist—a delicate swirl of leaves and stars.

“This will call me, should the need arise.”

Then, with a final shimmer, she vanishes into the leaves, her presence fading like dew at dawn.

You gather the cloud nettle root, carefully wrapping it in cloth, and begin the walk back to the healer’s cottage. The grove feels calmer now. Grateful.

By the time you return, morning has deepened into late day. Healer Aranel waits just outside her door, hanging bundles of dried sage in the sun.

“You were gone longer than expected,” she says, turning to you with a knowing look.

You explain what happened—the glowing moss, the fairy, the hollow, the moonseed warding. Her expression doesn’t change, but her eyes shimmer with something between recognition and respect.

“So, the old magic stirs even in the quiet corners,” she murmurs. “And you… you are beginning to walk between what is and what was.”

She takes the cloud nettle root from your hands, brushes your wrist gently, and glances at the faint silver marking.

“This is a sign. Not of power—but of promise. You are remembered, even by those who live beyond the Veil of memory.”

She turns and walks inside, beckoning you to follow.

“Come. There’s still more to do before the day ends. And more you need to know… before your path truly begins.”

Inside, Healer Aranel places three herbs before you. Each glows faintly, reacting to the mark on your wrist. She smiles.

“Choose one. Each leaf carries a lesson from the ancient roots of the world.”
