~ 🧚 The Hidden Glade and the Fairy's Request ~

You crouch low beneath the canopy, the light thinning to a gentle green hush as you approach the fern-covered stream bank. The pendant at your neck glows softly, in rhythm with the trickling water.

You recognize the cloud nettle immediately—tall, with silver-veined leaves and a faint purple bloom at the base. You draw the forager’s blade and dig gently at its roots, careful not to sever them.

But just as your blade touches the soil, the air changes.

A soft trill—like the sound of wind through a flute—floats through the grove.

You rise, slowly.

Across the stream, perched on the rim of a twisted flower bulb, hovers a creature of light and thread and shimmer. Wings like silk, hair like spun copper, eyes deep as starlit pools.

A fairy.

“You take what grows,” she says, voice delicate and sure, “but do you know what it cost to bloom?”

You open your mouth, unsure of what to say.

She lifts her chin.

“This grove is sacred to us. We let the root grow here, though its power tempts many. But lately…” she pauses, expression dimming, “...a sickness spreads from the eastern hollow. Something is waking beneath the soil that should not.”

She flutters closer, hovering just out of reach.

“ - I do not ask you to fight, human. But if you would earn the root truly, help us. Place one of our moonseed wards along the hollow’s edge. It will not drive the dark back—but it may keep it sleeping a little longer.”

She drifts down to a mossy stone and opens a pouch woven from leaf and light. Inside are three glowing seeds.

The fairy meets your gaze, her expression suddenly softer.

“ - You are not just a boy of Eritea. The land knows it. The Veil stirs. Even here, in silence, the world waits.”
