~ 🐟 Story Scene: The Scales Beneath the Surface ~

Leaving the forge behind, the hum of the pendant slowly settles, but your thoughts do not. Everywhere you look, the market feels stranger than before—not because it has changed, but because you have.

The path winds past the baker’s stall, past jars of glowing amber honey and stacks of golden loaves, until you’re met with a sudden burst of sea-scented air.

A long table lined with chipped blue tile sits under a tattered sailcloth canopy. Bowls of water glisten in the sun, each holding a different kind of fish—some familiar, others… impossible.

One has scales like silver leaves.

Another, fins that pulse with bioluminescence.

A third blinks at you with two pairs of eyes.

Behind the stall stands Old Fenna, the fishmonger. You know her only by the sound of her voice at dawn and the way her boots slosh when she walks through town.

She watches you approach with a crooked smile and says:

“ - Not every mystery lies in the sky, child. Some sleep below the surface, waiting to be remembered.”

She reaches under the table and pulls out a net made of glass-thread. Inside, a shell pulses with a faint glow.

“ - This one’s not for sale,” she says. “But if you can answer the river’s memory… it might open itself to you.”

Old Fenna gestures to three water basins, each containing a curious aquatic creature:

🐠 A shimmering riverfish with translucent fins – freshwater knowledge
🐙 A blue-shelled cephalopod curling over stones – oceanic mystery
🐊 A strange lizardlike creature resting half-submerged – amphibians and aquatic evolution
