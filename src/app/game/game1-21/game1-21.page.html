~ 🌾 The Missing Root ~

You gently lift the bundle your mother requested, wrapping it into the satchel with care. But just as you’re about to thank Healer Aranel and leave, her brow furrows.

She moves to a shelf in the far corner, lifting the lid of a small clay jar.

“- Hm,” she murmurs. “No scent. That’s not right.”

She looks back at you, her fingers brushing lightly across a set of small parchment labels.

“ - Your father’s ointment,” she says. “He’s been using it for his joints, yes? I made the last batch with cloud nettle root—but the stores are empty. And the new delivery hasn’t come down from the hills.”

She sighs, then smiles—calm, but expectant.

“If you have time, would you fetch it for me? You remember the path—east, toward the stream past the willow break. It only grows in shaded places where the water sings and the trees forget to speak.”

She presses a small wooden blade into your hand—a forager’s knife, etched with runes to guide you.

“Bring back only what calls to you. The root hides if it senses greed.”

You nod and turn to go, the satchel on your back now carrying not just herbs but intent.

“Oh—and one more thing,” she calls after you.
“Be careful where the moss glows blue. That means you’re close… but not alone.”

As you step into the outer grove, sunlight dapples the leaves. You come across three natural features, each seemingly humming with unseen presence:

🪨 A ring of mossy stones – Roots grow deep in shaded soil between them
🌳 A hollow tree filled with blue mushrooms – Strange but fragrant, pulsing faintly in the dark
🌾 A fern-covered stream bank – Glints of water and spidery roots trail like veins in the mud
