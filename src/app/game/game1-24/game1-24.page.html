~ 🍯 The Hive’s Request ~

The sun hangs low in the sky by the time you return home, a quiet orange glow stretching over the rooftops of Lorynth.

Inside the cottage, your mother hums softly as she stirs a pot of broth. Your father sits by the hearth, rubbing his knees absently—his usual chair wrapped in a woven blanket.

You approach and hand him the small jar of ointment from Healer Aranel. He takes it with a grunt of thanks, twisting the lid with careful hands.

“Smells strong. She’s good at what she does, that Aranel,” he says. “Should help with the joints before the cold sets in.”

He leans back, letting the warmth of the fire settle into his bones. Then he looks at you and raises an eyebrow.

“Since you’re still full of energy, how about one more errand?” he says with a smirk. “We’re out of honey. The hive in the grove behind the cottage should have plenty this time of year.”

You nod, grabbing a small wooden pot and setting off once again, this time toward the edge of the property where the grass gives way to wildflowers and the sweet hum of bees fills the air.

The hive is nestled in an old, hollow tree. Golden light spills from the cracks in the bark like liquid sunlight. The air is warm, thick with the scent of clover and pollen.

You approach slowly, as your father taught you, careful and calm. But just as you reach to collect the honeycomb, a sudden silence falls over the grove.

Then—**she** appears.

Not crawling from the hive, but **rising** from it—an elegant figure formed of golden light and glimmering wings, with eyes like drops of amber and a voice that thrums in the bones.

“You would take from the hive,” she says, not unkindly. “But the hive, too, has need.”

You step back, heart pounding. She hovers in the air, regal and strange. The **Queen Bee**.

“A shadow has crept into our foraging grounds,” she continues. “Something spoils the nectar, dulls the song of the flowers. We do not ask for battle—but for aid. Will you help us restore the bloom?”

You nod slowly. You’ve never spoken with a bee—let alone a queen. But something about her presence feels ancient, like it echoes the same strange familiarity as the pendant around your neck.

“Then choose how you will help us,” she says, and extends three glowing drops of honey—each floating before you like golden stars.

Each honey drop pulses with a different light, representing a different path of help:

🍯 Sun-Drop – Strengthen the flowers: leads to trivia about plant biology and pollination
🐝 Moon-Drop – Track the corrupted nectar: opens questions about environmental science and ecosystems
🌼 Star-Drop – Communicate with other hives: reveals quiz questions about social insects and animal behavior
