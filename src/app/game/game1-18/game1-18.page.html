~ 🏺 The Clay That Holds the Shape ~

With sweet warmth lingering on your tongue, you step away from Gramma Thistle’s cart, ready—perhaps—for the first time today, to finally return home.

The sun dips lower behind the hills, casting long amber shadows across the cobblestone streets. Merchants begin to pack their goods. Children chase each other between the stalls with the last of their festival sweets. The town breathes a little slower now.

Then you hear it—a deep, rhythmic thud, soft as a heartbeat in wet earth.

You turn the corner near the edge of the square and find Tiren, your friend since childhood, seated at his small pottery wheel beneath a faded canvas tent. His stall is smaller than most, but he keeps it spotless—shelves lined with clay vessels, painted plates, and delicate cups that shimmer with blue glaze.

“- You’re still out here?” you ask.

He grins, his hands already caked in clay.

“ - Market never ends if the work hasn’t.” He pauses, studying your face. “You look… different.”

You smile faintly, tired.

"I am."

Tiren nods like he understands more than he says, then gestures to a nearby stool.

“Sit. Talk. Let your head settle while your feet rest.”

You do. The wheel spins, and he shapes a lump of clay into something resembling a jar. Steam rises from a nearby kettle. The scent of warm chamomile and earth floats between you.

“My father says clay remembers the hands that shaped it,” Tiren murmurs. “Sometimes I wonder… if we’re made of the same stuff.”

He sets the jar aside and reaches into a wooden box, pulling out three tiny ceramic tokens, each no bigger than a coin. He sets them on the bench beside you.

“I made these while thinking of you. Back before anything changed.”

You pick one up. It’s warm.
