~ 🔪 Story Scene: The Weight of the Blade ~

You leave Old Fenna’s stall with a mind teeming with currents and coral—visions of creatures both known and long-forgotten still shimmering in the corner of your thoughts. The pendant lies still against your chest, though you feel a deep vibration, like it’s bracing for what’s next.

The stone path takes a turn toward the outer ring of the market, where bright colors and fragrant spices give way to iron hooks, thick gloves, and the scent of salt and blood.

The butcher’s stall looms beneath a red awning stained darker by time. There are no displays here—only slabs of oak and steel. Ropes of sausage hang like garlands, and strips of meat glisten beside wrapped bundles marked with chalk symbols.

Branrik, the butcher, is carving a side of boar when you approach. His arms are thick as tree trunks, his apron soaked in a day's worth of labor.

He looks up with a nod—not friendly, not unkind. Just real.

“ - You’re late,” he grunts. “Your mother wanted a roast and marrow cuts. You’ll carry them back yourself.”

He wraps the meat in waxed cloth, weighing it expertly. Then his eyes narrow.

“ - You’ve got the look of someone who’s seen through something.” He leans in, lowering his voice. “Dreams don’t feed a family, boy. Steel does. Blood does. But even those… come with a price.”

He lays the bundle down, but keeps his hand on it.

“ - Tell me something. You ever wonder what must be given for a meal like this to exist? The stories told in blood and bone?”

His eyes gleam.

“Want to hear what the beasts remember?”

He pulls out three carved bone tokens from a jar behind the stall. Each one is shaped like a creature’s tooth.

“Pick one. Each bite holds memory, if you’ve the mind to chew it.”

🐗 Boar’s tusk – Knowledge of wild beasts and foraging creatures
🐺 Wolf fang – Predators, instincts, survival lore
🐄 Ox horn shard – Domesticated animals, farming, and trade
