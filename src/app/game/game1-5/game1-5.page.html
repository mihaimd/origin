~ Mira the Greengrower ~

You stop by Mira the Greengrower’s cart, as you always have. Her hands are stained from peeling rinds, her smile warm and worn.

- Ah, the boy returns,” she says, tossing a ripe plum into your satchel. “Your parents keeping well?

You nod, distracted by a shimmer—a flicker of memory. A market not of wood and rope, but of metal and mist, where fruits glowed with inner fire and sellers whispered in tongues unknown. A vision? A dream?

No

A memory

📜 A city of lost scrolls and stone scholars
🐉 A burning battlefield echoing with ancient war cries
🌿 A grove older than the sun, guarded by whispering trees
