~ 🎪 Story Scene: The Dance of Air and Flame ~

The lemonade slides down your throat like liquid sunlight—cool and bright and strangely grounding. You exhale, for what feels like the first time that day.

You glance to the side, just as a ripple of applause rises from the village square.

There, framed by the colorful streamers strung between food carts and hanging lanterns, is a circle of space cleared for performance. A few crates mark the edge of the makeshift stage, and standing in the center are two figures: a tall man in a crimson vest, and beside him, a small girl no older than ten.

The girl grins, hands outstretched, her fingers tipped with silver caps. With a sudden movement, she flips backward onto her hands, balancing perfectly as her father tosses her a pair of flaming rings.

She catches them mid-air without even looking.

Lira leans toward you, still sipping her own cup.

“ - That’s Kael and Anya. They’ve performed here since before I was born. Said they were just passing through one day… but never left.”

The father—Kael—bows low. Anya vaults onto his shoulders, balancing on one foot. The fire rings spin around her, leaving trails of gold in the air.

The crowd claps, enchanted. And you… you feel something stir.

Not a vision. Not a memory. But a yearning. The way they move—like wind and rhythm, like freedom.

You blink—and for the briefest moment, Anya’s eyes flash silver, and she’s looking straight at you.

She winks.

Then flips down, lands lightly, and the crowd erupts in cheer.

Kael walks toward the edge of the circle, his daughter now perched on one arm.

“ - We were meant to entertain,” he says with a smile. “But those who really watch… they remember.”

He holds up three small juggling balls. Each glows faintly—blue, green, and gold.

“Pick one. Let’s see if your mind dances like your thoughts do.”
