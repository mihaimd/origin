~ The Eye Behind the Silk ~

As the coin cools in your palm, the old man simply nods. He doesn’t speak again. He only reaches under the table, pulls out a small square of folded silk, and slides it toward you.

You’ll know when to open it,” he says, his voice like dry leaves. “But not before.

You reach for it—
—and blink.

He’s gone.

Only the coins remain, scattered across the empty table like ancient stars spilled from the night.

You slip the silk bundle into your satchel beside the glowing fruit and move on, your mind spinning.
