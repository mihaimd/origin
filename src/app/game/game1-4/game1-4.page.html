~ 🍎 The Market of Lorynth ~

The dawn breaks slowly over Eriterra, casting a golden hush upon the rooftops of Lorynth. The sky is pale blue, the kind that promises warmth, but your heart remains a tangle of cold thoughts.

The pendant lies quiet against your chest, its faint pulse from the night before gone still. You don’t know whether to feel relieved or abandoned.

Your mother’s voice calls softly through the cottage door.

“Before you vanish into your thoughts again, love, take this coin pouch and fetch some fruit and root for supper. We’ll need them before the market clears.”

You hesitate. After all that happened, going to the market feels… trivial. But the ordinary has a strange gravity. Perhaps it will ground you.
