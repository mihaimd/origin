~ 🔥 The Forge That Remembers ~

The crowd thins as you move past the stonemason’s booth. Behind a curtain of thick smoke and the ringing of steel, you find yourself at the edge of the Forge of Jorek, Lorynth’s blacksmith and quiet giant of a man.

The forge is alive—its breath loud and fiery, casting waves of heat that dance across your skin. Sparks leap and vanish like fireflies meeting their end.

Jorek stands behind the anvil, hammering iron into shape with a rhythm older than language.

He doesn’t glance up, but somehow, he knows.

"- You’ve seen things now, haven’t you?"

You hesitate, then nod. The pendant stirs faintly against your chest, pulsing like a second heartbeat.

"Aye,” Jorek mutters, pulling the metal from the flame. “When the Veil begins to thin, some remember. And some are remembered."

He plunges the iron into a trough of water. Steam howls up.

"What you carry isn't meant to stay unchanged. Memory, like steel, needs shaping."

He gestures toward a heavy wooden workbench nearby. Upon it sit three raw metal forms—unfinished, waiting. Nearby lies a toolkit of memory-forging instruments, etched with arcane symbols. You’ve never seen anything like them.

You’ve come from elsewhere, whether you know it or not. The world will soon ask who you are. But first… you must choose what you become.

Jorek slides the metal blanks toward you.
