~ The Eye Behind the Silk ~

The crowd thickens as midday nears. You hear the clang of hammer on steel from the far edge of the square, where the blacksmith’s forge sends plumes of dark smoke into the sky. The smell of molten iron mixes with cinnamon and sweetwine from nearby stalls.

You pass a masked juggler tossing stones shaped like moons.

A woman sells crystal insects in glass jars that hum ancient lullabies.

You begin to wonder:

Was the market always like this… or are you simply seeing it now for what it truly is?
