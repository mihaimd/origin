~ 🌊 The Whispering Lake ~

The morning sun glints silver on the still waters of Lake Viralis, casting long reflections of sky and tree upon the surface. Mist curls low along the shore, and the wind carries the scent of lilies and cool stone.

Your feet press softly into the muddy banks as you crouch beside the water’s edge. Healer Aranel had mentioned a rare aquatic herb—skyroot frond—which blooms only beneath the water’s surface in the morning light.

You roll up your sleeves and reach into the shimmering water, fingers brushing the reeds. The water is colder than expected, deep and endless, as though the lake itself is watching you.

A ripple breaks the surface. Then another.

Suddenly, a song—wordless, haunting—rises from the mist.

You pull your hand back quickly and rise to your feet as a figure emerges from the lake. Not walking, not swimming—but gliding just beneath the surface.

She is unlike anything you've ever seen—her skin is pale as pearl, hair flowing like riverweed, and eyes shimmering with shifting blues and greens. A mermaid.

She watches you in silence for a moment, then speaks, her voice like the echo of water in a cave.

“You reach for the frond of the lake… but the lake reaches back.”

You don’t know whether to speak or run, but her gaze holds you steady—not threatening, but solemn.

“Few walk this shore with the mark of memory,” she says, glancing to the faint silver symbol still glowing on your wrist. “And fewer still can hear the songs of the deep.”

She dips beneath the surface and rises again, holding a small cluster of glowing skyroot fronds, their tips dripping silver light.

“I will give these to you. But in return, I ask your aid.”

You step closer, curiosity outweighing fear.

“There are questions hidden in the depths,” she whispers. “Traces of the world before the Veil. I will give you a piece of the song if you answer one of our riddles.”

She lifts her hand, and three bubbles rise from the water—each glowing with a different light, each pulsing with a quiet sound.

Each bubble holds a question from the old world, tied to knowledge long forgotten. Choose one to answer:

🌊 Blue Bubble – Reveals a trivia memory about oceans and aquatic creatures
🌿 Green Bubble – Reveals a trivia memory about water-based plants and healing herbs
🔱 Golden Bubble – Reveals a trivia memory about myths and sea legends
